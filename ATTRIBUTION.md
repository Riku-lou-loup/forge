When redistributing FORGE or covered derivative works, follow section 4 of the
license. Provide a copy of the license, identify modified files, and preserve
applicable copyright and attribution notices. Carry forward the applicable
attribution from [NOTICE](NOTICE):

A readable copy of the applicable NOTICE attribution can be included in a
distributed NOTICE file, accompanying source/documentation, or an appropriate
notices display, as permitted by the license. No homepage banner is required.

## Private use and hosted services

Running FORGE privately or as a hosted-only service without distributing copies
does not by itself trigger section 4's redistribution requirements. A visible
credit such as "Based on FORGE by Dang Duong Dang Khoa" is welcome but optional.
